Ready.

  1. Open Ableton Live (or your DAW). If brEARk doesn't show up, rescan: Live > Preferences (Settings in Live 12) > Plug-Ins > Rescan (VST3: turn on “Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders”; Audio Units: turn on “Use Audio Units”).
  2. Put brEARk on the master track.
  3. Press play. After a few seconds brEARk asks where you listen and how loud it is. One click each.
