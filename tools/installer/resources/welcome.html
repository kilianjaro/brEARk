brEARk.

A break coach for your ears. brEARk sits on your master channel, measures what you hear, and tells you when to take a break.

This installs the plugin for all users:

  • VST3 in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

brEARk never changes your audio (except HARD mode's power-down, when you choose it), adds no latency and never connects to the internet.

brEARk is free software under the GNU AGPLv3. It's a coach, not a medical device.
